# September 2, 2026 card batch

Last verified: 2026-09-02

- Red Rider `70083` exposes its three Frost deaths as a visible Countdown while
  it is in the deck. Each eligible enemy death under Biting Frost decrements the
  Countdown; round cleanup does not. At zero, reset it to 3 even if no row has
  room, then attempt the existing summon.
- Sir Scratch-a-Lot `70177` reacts to allied Beast appearances only from hand
  or deck. A copy already on the battlefield still performs its deploy Full
  Moon Boost, but does not gain the appearance Boost. Hybrid `70176` offers
  only allied Bronze units carrying `HideTag.Deathwish`; a locked chosen target
  still does not resolve its Deathwish.
- Ihuarraquax `12018` lets its owner select up to three enemy units for the
  seven-damage resolution instead of choosing randomly. The September 2 Draug
  selection change was split on September 7: original `22002` is random again,
  while Northern Realms `70197` selects up to eight cemetery units, capped by
  free row space, then transforms them into 1-power Draugirs and resurrects them.
- Morenn `53006` Banishes only a unit actually destroyed by her seven damage.
  The lethal damage path is queued, so mark the prospective target Doomed in
  the queued damage action and restore its prior Doomed state when it survives;
  checking or Banish-ing only after `Damage` is not pipeline-safe. Morenn:
  Forest Child `52009` cancels the opponent's next Special regardless of
  rarity.
- Reinforced Trebuchet `44008` Strengthens itself by 1 for each ordinary Crew
  trigger on deploy; its owner-turn-end random damage is unchanged. Wraith
  Sorcerer `70194` uses current total power (`CardPoint`) for its 3-power lock
  threshold, so a 2-base copy generated by Congregation Cleric correctly locks
  after receiving a 1-point Boost.
- Plumard `70147` is player-facing “吸血鸟怪” and its Chinese rule uses the
  requested “汲取” terminology. Feast of Blood `70183` names the generated
  token with Chinese quotation marks. Detlaff: Crimson Curse `70102` keeps its
  existing effect and receives only the clarified unit wording.
- Artorius Vigo `70103` has 7 base power. Syanna `70025` decrements her visible
  two-turn Countdown at the start of her owner's turns and resolves her current
  wounded amount when the Countdown reaches zero; turn-end events do not advance
  her Countdown.
